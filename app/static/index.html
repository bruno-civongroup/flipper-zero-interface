Listen for Sub-GHz signals (remotes, garage doors, weather stations, doorbells).

Select a frequency and duration, then click "Listen". Uses the Flipper's built-in CC1101 radio. Press a remote or trigger a device during the listen window to capture its signal.
Read NFC tags (13.56 MHz) or RFID cards (125 kHz).

Select the type, click "Scan", then hold the tag/card against the back of the Flipper. NFC reads badges, transit cards, NTAG stickers, Mifare, and phone NFC. RFID reads keycards, EM4100, HID ProxCards, and other 125 kHz cards.

WiFi scanning via Marauder (ESP32 Dev Board over UART).

Make sure your Flipper is connected above, then click "Scan APs" to find nearby networks. Scans take ~10 seconds as commands are routed through the Flipper's UART to the ESP32.
